CPR+ / VPN Blackhole

Why is my VPN blocked on this network?

OptumCare Network Access — VPN Blackhole Protection

What just happened?

When you connected to a corporate network (office WiFi or wired), the system detected that you are on-premises and temporarily blocked VPN connections to your corporate headend. This is intentional and by design.

Why does this exist?

This protection prevents "hairpinning." Without it, your laptop can send all internet traffic into the VPN tunnel, route it to a datacentre, and then back out to the internet — even when you are physically on a corporate network. That path wastes bandwidth, increases latency, and can slow your connection significantly. VPN Blackhole prevents that automatically.

Do I need to do anything?

No. This process is automatic. When you leave the office and connect from home, a hotel, or another off-site network, the block is removed automatically. You do not need to restart anything.

Technical reference

Show technical details for IT support and curious users
  • The block is implemented via a managed hosts file entry (vpn_blocked.flag signals active state).
  • The subsystem is called VPN Blackhole and is part of the CPR+ framework.
  • It is controlled by the BlackholeEnabled configuration flag.
  • Set-VpnHostsEntry.ps1 manages the actual hosts file entries.
  • The block is removed automatically on every non-on-prem network transition.
